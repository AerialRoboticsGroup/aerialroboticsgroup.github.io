# Join the Aerial Robotics Group

We are looking for people who want to build aerial robots and fly them. Our work spans aerial physical interaction and perching,
soft and bio-inspired aerial robots, learning-based sensing and control, and aerial robots for environmental monitoring and
manufacturing. The group is based in the School of Civil, Aerospace and Design Engineering at the University of Bristol and works
within the [Bristol Robotics Laboratory](https://www.bristolroboticslab.com/).

Before you write to us, read two or three of our [recent papers](/publications/) and tell us where you would take them.
A short, specific message gets a reply; a message that could have been sent to any group does not.

## PhD students

We take on a small number of PhD students each year, and funding is competitive. The main routes are:

- **Faculty scholarships.** The Faculty of Science and Engineering awards University of Bristol Postgraduate Research
  Scholarships and EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Award studentships in one round each year, with a deadline in January for a start
  the following autumn. They cover fees, a stipend at the UKRI rate and a research allowance. Details and eligibility are on the
  [Faculty's scholarship page](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/science-engineering/postgraduate-research/pgr-scholarships/).
- **China Scholarship Council.** Bristol offers joint
  [CSC scholarships](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/students/support/finances/scholarships/china-scholarship-council/) for applicants from China.
- **Project studentships.** When a funded project includes a studentship, it is advertised on the University's
  [jobs page](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/jobs/) and announced here.
- **Your own funding.** Scholarships from your government, employer or home institution, or self-funding.

**How to apply.** Email Dr Kocer with the subject line "PhD enquiry: your name" and attach:

1. a CV and your degree transcripts;
2. a one-page research statement: which of our themes interests you, what you would do, and why with us;
3. the funding route you have in mind and its deadline;
4. the names of two referees, and a thesis, report or paper you wrote if you have one.

If there is a fit we will arrange a call. You then apply formally to the
[PhD in Aerospace Engineering](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/research/aerospace-engineering/), naming Dr Kocer
as your proposed supervisor, in time for the scholarship deadline. Allow several weeks before that deadline for the formal
application and references.

**What we look for.** A strong degree in aerospace, mechanical or electrical engineering, robotics, computer science or physics;
hands-on experience with real robots or drones, control and estimation, PX4 or ROS, or machine learning; and evidence that you
finish things: projects, code, a thesis or a paper. The programme page lists the formal entry and English language requirements.

## Postdoctoral researchers

**Funded positions** arise when a research project includes one. They are advertised on the University's
[jobs page](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/jobs/) and announced here, with the application details in the advert.
We are not able to answer speculative enquiries about funded positions that do not exist yet.

**Fellowships.** We welcome enquiries from researchers who want to develop an externally funded fellowship hosted in the group.
Routes include:

- [Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowships](https://raeng.org.uk/programmes-and-prizes/programmes/uk-grants-and-prizes/support-for-research/research-fellowships/)
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)
- [Royal Society Newton International Fellowships](https://royalsociety.org/grants/newton-international/) (for researchers currently outside the UK)
- [Royal Society University Research Fellowships](https://royalsociety.org/grants/university-research/)
  and [Dorothy Hodgkin Fellowships](https://www.royalsociety.org/grants/dorothy-hodgkin-fellowship/)
- [Leverhulme Early Career Fellowships](https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/early-career-fellowships)
- [Royal Commission for the Exhibition of 1851 Research Fellowships](https://royalcommission1851.org/fellowships/research-fellowships)
- UKRI schemes, including EPSRC fellowships and Future Leaders Fellowships

Host-institution support takes time to arrange, so contact us at least two months before the funder's deadline. Email Dr Kocer with
the subject line "Fellowship: your name" and attach a CV with publication list, a one-page outline of the research you would propose
and how it connects with ours, and the scheme and deadline you are targeting.

## Visiting researchers and interns

We welcome visiting PhD students and researchers who bring their own funding (for example a CSC visiting scholarship, Erasmus+ or
a grant from your home institution) for stays of three to twelve months, when there is a clear joint project. Write to us three to
six months ahead: the University's visitor and visa paperwork takes time.

We occasionally host interns when a project and funding exist. We cannot normally host unfunded interns and do not reply to
speculative internship requests.

## Bristol students

Final-year and MSc projects in aerial robotics are offered through the School's project allocation each year. If you would like to
shape a project, talk to Dr Kocer early, ideally the term before projects are chosen.
